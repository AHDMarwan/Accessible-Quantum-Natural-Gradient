# AQNG manuscript source policy

This manuscript is a clean rewrite. No prose is copied or adapted from the previous `paper/aqng/` manuscript.

Scientific inputs permitted for the rewrite are:

1. the committed frozen AQNG result package under `results/paper/paper_scale_v2/`;
2. the AQNG implementation and experiment code in this repository;
3. the prior-paper lineage listed in `references.bib`;
4. additional primary literature checked explicitly during writing.

The earlier AQNG manuscript may be consulted only as a historical artifact, not as source text. Numerical claims must be regenerated or checked against the committed v2 tables. The unpublished spectral-geometry manuscript is cited temporarily as an unpublished manuscript until a permanent identifier is available.
